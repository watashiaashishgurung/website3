Welcome to the guessing game! Here are the instructions:

  • Enter the number of players and click "Next".
  • Enter the names of the players and click "Start Game".
  • Each player makes a guess in each round until they win a round, then their guess is defaulted to the winner's guess.
  • The player with the lowest unique guess wins the round.
  • The game continues until only one player remains eligible.
  • The winner is the player with the highest amount at the end.
